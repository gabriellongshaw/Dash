# Dash

A fast, modern endless runner. Jump, slide and double jump through obstacles, collect coins, and spend them on colours, characters, hats and worlds. Play it in the classic side view, or in third person.

## How to play

Run as far as you can. Coins add 5 points each and distance adds the rest. The game keeps getting harder the further you go.

**Desktop:** `Space`, `↑` or `W` to jump (press again in the air to double jump). Hold `↓` or `S` to slide, or to fast-fall in the air. `P` or `Esc` pauses.

**Touch:** tap the screen or the jump button to jump, and hold the slide button to slide.

### Third person

Press **Play**, then pick **Third person** on the view screen before you start, to run down a three-lane track with the camera behind your runner, like Subway Surfers. Your choice is remembered on that device.

**Desktop:** `←` `→` or `A` `D` change lane. `Space`, `↑` or `W` jump (again in the air to double jump). `↓` or `S` slide. `P` or `Esc` pauses.

**Touch:** swipe left or right to change lane, up to jump and down to slide. Each swipe does one thing, so a long drag will not change several lanes. A quick tap also jumps.

Coins, quests and the shop are shared between both modes, but third person has its own best score and its own leaderboard. Score quests only count Classic runs. Obstacles are the same as in Classic, but they sit in lanes, so many can be dodged by switching lane as well as by jumping or sliding. Wide barriers, ceiling gates, force fields and wide spikes cover every lane and still need a jump or slide. Your character, colours and hat are visible from behind, and so is the world you picked. The sides of the track are lined with scenery from your world, like cacti in Desert or lollipops in Candy.

### Obstacles

- **Crates and spikes:** jump over them.
- **Tall walls:** too high for one jump, so double jump.
- **Ceiling gates:** slide underneath.
- **Drones:** hover low. Slide under them or jump over. Some bob up and down.
- **Rollers:** fast wheels that arrive sooner than they look.
- **Buzzsaws:** blades that roll toward you along the ground and get faster the further you run. Jump over them.
- **Bouncing orbs:** spiked balls that hop along the ground. Jump when they are low, slip underneath when they are high.
- **Missiles:** fast rockets announced by a warning triangle at the edge of the screen. Jump low ones and slide under higher ones.
- **Force fields:** wide barriers that need one long jump.
- **Trains (third person):** long carriages that fill a lane. You cannot jump or slide them, so switch lane early. Some roll toward you.

## Customise

Coins from every run go into your wallet. Spend them in the shop on:

- **Colours:** 34, from Emerald to an animated rainbow Prism.
- **Characters:** 19, including Bot, Slime, Frog, Ninja, Fox, Ghost, Dino, Alien and Snowman. Any character works with any colour and hat.
- **Hats:** 29, from a sprout to a unicorn horn.
- **Worlds:** 17, including Skyline, Desert, Arctic, Neon, Volcano, Space, Matrix and Crystal Cave. Each has its own scenery, weather and obstacle colours.

Backgrounds change as you run. The sky moves through day, dusk and night, and stars and auroras fade in.

## Quests

Quests are another way to earn coins. Open **Quests** from the home screen.

- **Daily:** four quests that reset every day, worth 25, 45, 75 and 110 coins as they get harder.
- **Weekly:** three bigger quests that reset on Monday, worth 125 to 250 coins each.
- Progress counts automatically as you play. Tap **Claim** when a bar is full.

## Leaderboard

See the top 25 runners **This week** and **All time**, ranked by best score. Switch between **Classic** and **Third person** to see each board. Anyone can view it, and guests can tap a name to open that player's profile (unless they have limited who can see it). Sign in with Google to put your score on it. You appear as your first name and last initial, and your profile photo shows beside it if you keep your photo set to **Public**. You can hide yourself from the leaderboard at any time, which also removes your entries.

## Friends and profiles

Sign in with Google to use Friends.

- **Username and photo:** pick a unique username, add an optional display name, and use your Google photo, upload your own, or have none.
- **Add friends:** search by name or username, send a request, and they accept from their Friends page. You can have up to 50 friends.
- **Gifts:** send a friend coins, an item, or both.
- **Favourites and blocking:** favourite up to 10 friends so they sit at the top of your list. Blocking someone hides you from them completely, and they are never told.

## Polls and feedback

Open **Polls** from the home screen. Filter by All, Polls, Feedback or Suggestions.

- **Polls:** posted by Dash's creator. Anyone can see the results. Sign in with Google to vote. Change your vote or tap **Undo vote** (or tap your choice again) to take it back while the poll is open. Only the creator can see who voted for what.
- **Feedback and suggestions:** written by players. Sign in with Google and choose a username, then tap **New post**. Posts are public and show your username. You can post 3 times a day and have up to 10 posts at once, and you can edit or delete your own posts.
- **Replies:** every post has a **Reply** button that opens its thread. Replies are public and show your username. Guests can read them, but you need to sign in and have a username to reply. You can reply 20 times a day, each reply can be 300 characters, and you can delete your own. The author of a feedback or suggestion post can also delete replies on it.
- **Moderation:** the creator can edit their own polls, delete any post or reply, and close or reopen polls. Guests can read everything but have to sign in to vote, post or reply.

## Privacy and your account

- **Guests:** you can play straight away as a guest. Your coins, items and quests are saved to an anonymous account in your browser. Signing in with Google keeps all of it and lets you carry on across devices.
- **What others can see:** from your profile you choose who can open your **profile** at all (Everyone including guests, which is the default, Signed-in players or Friends only), who can see your **photo** (Public, Signed-in players, Friends only or Only me), and who can see your **stats** and **appearance** (Everyone, Friends only or Only me). You also choose who can send you friend requests, and whether you appear in search and on the leaderboard.
- **Fair play:** runs are checked by the game's server, so coins, scores and quest rewards can't be edited from the browser.

## Ads

Dash is free and supported by ads. A full-screen ad can appear when you press Play again, and you can optionally watch one on the game-over screen to double the coins from that run. See [privacy](privacy/index.html) for details.

## Sound

The music and sound effects are made in your browser, so there is nothing to download. Use the speaker button to switch music and effects on or off separately. Browsers only allow sound after a tap or key press, so music starts once you first tap or press a key.
